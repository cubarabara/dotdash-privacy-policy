DotDash — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how DotDash ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use our Morse code learning application. DotDash is developed by Cubaraba Dynamics.

1. Data We Collect Directly

DotDash does not require an account and does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our own servers. Your learning progress, chapter completion, statistics, and app settings are stored locally on your device only (using Android's local storage), and are permanently deleted if you uninstall the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

DotDash is a free app supported by advertising. We use Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads. To serve, personalize, and measure these ads, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect:

  • Advertising ID (a resettable, non-permanent device identifier)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, screen size)
  • IP address (used to derive an approximate, city-level location — we do not access precise/GPS location)
  • App interaction data (ad impressions, clicks, and general in-app engagement relevant to ad delivery)

We do not have access to or control over how Google processes this data beyond what is described here. For details on how Google collects and uses data through AdMob, see Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner sites/apps.

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, or another region with similar requirements, you may be shown a consent choice before personalized ads are served, in accordance with applicable law.

3. Analytics & Crash Reporting

We do not use any analytics or crash-reporting tools that collect personal information. The app does not track your behavior beyond what is described in Section 2 for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to third parties, beyond what is required for Google AdMob to function as described above.

5. Data Security

Since we do not operate our own backend servers for this app, there is no centrally-stored personal data for us to protect. Data collected by Google AdMob is transmitted using industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) and is governed by Google's own security practices.

6. Children's Privacy

DotDash is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us using the details below so we can address it.

7. Your Choices

You can reset or limit ad personalization at any time via your device's system settings (Android Settings → Google → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization", depending on your Android version).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
